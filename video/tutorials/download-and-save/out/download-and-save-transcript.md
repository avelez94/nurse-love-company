# How to download and save your goodies

Nurse Love Company tutorial. Length 3:25. No voiceover: everything below is shown on screen.

## 00:00 How to download and save your goodies

How to download and save your goodies. Sign in, download your file once, save it, and find it again.

## 00:06 Step 1: Sign in to your account (On a computer)

Click the person icon at the top right of any page.

## 00:14 Step 1: Sign in to your account (On a computer)

Enter your email and password.

Then click Sign in.

## 00:21 Step 1: Sign in to your account (On a phone)

Tap the menu button at the top left.

## 00:28 Step 1: Sign in to your account (On a phone)

Tap Sign in.

## 00:34 Step 1: Sign in to your account (On a phone)

Enter your email and password, then tap Sign in.

## 00:41 Step 2: Open My downloads (Same on a phone)

Your account opens.

Choose the My downloads tab.

## 00:48 Step 3: Find your resource (Same on a phone)

Each purchase has its own card, with the order number and version.

Ready to save? It says 1 download available.

## 01:00 Step 4: Before you download

Each digital purchase includes one download of the latest version.

Starting the download uses it. Just viewing the page does not.

When a new version is released, you get one new download of it.

## 01:18 Step 4: Start your download (Same on a computer)

When you're ready to save it, click or tap Download on the card.

## 01:26 Step 4: Start your download (Same on a phone)

A reminder appears.

Read it, then click or tap Download now.

## 01:35 Step 5: Save the file (Same on a computer)

The website hands the file to your browser.

Your browser saves it on your device.

## 01:44 Step 5: Where your file goes

It depends on your device and settings. Usually:

Computer: Your Downloads folder. If your browser asks where to save it, pick a folder you'll remember.

iPhone or iPad: Safari saves it to the Files app, in Downloads. A Downloads button appears next to Safari's address bar.

Android: Chrome saves it to your Downloads. You'll find it in the Files app.

Tip: keep your inserts together in one folder, like one named Nurse Love.

## 02:16 Step 6: Find it again

Computer: Open File Explorer on Windows or Finder on a Mac. Go to Downloads, or the folder you chose.

iPhone or iPad: Open Files, tap Browse, then Downloads. Not there? Look in iCloud Drive.

Android: Open the Files app and look in Downloads. Or in Chrome, tap More, then Downloads.

## 02:38 Step 6: Check your file (Same on a phone)

My downloads now shows Download used and the date.

The website can't see your device, so it can't tell if the file saved.

Open the file once to make sure it's there.

## 02:55 Step 7: Need help? (Same on a computer)

File didn't save or won't open? Click or tap Report a download problem.

A person at the store reviews every report.

## 03:07 Step 7: Need help? (Same on a phone)

For anything else, open Support in your account and send a message.

You'll hear back by email.

## 03:18 You're all set

You're all set. Download once, save it somewhere easy to find, and open it to check.
