# REVIEW: How to download and save your goodies (on a computer)

- Screens: the real website, captured on a local copy with a synthetic customer (Sam Taylor), a synthetic order
  (NLC-10001) and a sample PDF. Only computer screens are used.
- Narration: Kokoro voice Heart (af_heart) at 0.95 speed. Every line appears as it is spoken; the cursor or
  fingertip lands about a second into the sentence that names the action. Captions follow the narration.
- Music: original, composed by template/audio/compose_music.py, about 13 LU under the voice.
- Accuracy: the one download rule comes before the download. Save and find locations come from
  Google Chrome Help (Downloads folder by default; File Explorer and Finder) and say
  "most" or "usually". The video says the website cannot tell whether the file saved.
- Checked: hyperframes check ok with no warnings; review sheets for every scene; cursor arrival frames.
- Not checked: listening (the sound was measured, not heard), playback on real devices, the production upload.
